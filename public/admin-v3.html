Stadium Event Configuration
Rule: If event is before 4 PM, the industrial zone (motors) retains normal power.
If after 4 PM, the industrial zone can be reduced (min 70% — motor threshold).
The PRE-EVENT phase starts automatically 30 min before start time.
—
Weather Scenario — Automatic BME680 Adaptation
The system automatically adjusts zones based on environmental conditions:
Heat (>35°C) → max ventilation, reduced lighting
Cold (<5°C) → max residential + street lighting
Humidity (>80%) → increased ventilation
Storm (<1000 hPa) → safety mode, max street lighting
Fair Weather (>1025 hPa) → outdoor energy saving mode
